`Badge` is a pill-shaped status / category tag. Amber `highlight` is the signature team tag (e.g. "Team website"); soft variants carry status; `dot` adds a leading status dot.

```jsx
<Badge variant="primary" dot>Actief</Badge>
<Badge variant="highlight">Team website</Badge>
<Badge variant="success" dot>Gewonnen</Badge>
<Badge variant="accent">Hot lead</Badge>
<Badge variant="neutral">+ 3</Badge>
```

- `variant`: primary · accent · highlight · highlight-soft · success · warning · danger · info · neutral · outline
- `dot`: leading status dot. Keep labels short (1–2 words).
